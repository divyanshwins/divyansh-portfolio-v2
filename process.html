Process

In short

I start with the problem statement, then go looking for evidence. Primary research — ethnographic studies, interviews, usability testing — sits alongside secondary reading. The quantitative half runs through Google Analytics, Meta, Metabase and Microsoft Clarity, then Excel or Python until the data says something specific. Only then do I design: user flows, information architecture, visual reference mapping. First renders come out of AI — Claude wired to Figma over MCP, or straight into React Native and HTML. Stakeholders review, I produce, we test, and the numbers decide whether it ships.

Quantify it

The qualitative half tells me where to look; the quantitative half tells me how much it matters. I pull customer response data out of the analytics stack, then push it through Excel or Python until it resolves into a specific inference — not a vibe.
